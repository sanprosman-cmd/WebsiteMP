Fusion Solar

Solar that manages itself

What It Is

Smart PV, from rooftop to farm

Huawei Fusion Solar delivers smart PV power generation across residential, commercial and utility scale — intelligent inverters, per-string optimization and cloud O&M, installed and maintained by our certified electrical teams.

Residential · C&I · Utility Smart string inverters Cloud O&M Grid & microgrid ready
Why It Matters

Why smart PV beats dumb panels

  • Per-string optimization recovers yield lost to shading and mismatch
  • Cloud O&M flags faults before they become downtime
  • One platform scales from a rooftop to a ground-mount farm
  • Grid codes and microgrid integration handled at the inverter level
What We Deliver

Technology plus everything around it

As an EPCC contractor, we deliver Fusion Solar as a producing plant — not boxes on a dock. Our scope wraps the technology with the engineering, civil and commissioning work that makes it generate.
