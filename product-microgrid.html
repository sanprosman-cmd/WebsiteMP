Product 01 · Huawei

Microgrid solution

One match for all microgrid needs — generation, storage, control and load engineered as a single system for sites the grid can't reach, or rates too high to depend on.

What It Is

A grid of your own, sized to your load

A microgrid combines solar generation, battery energy storage and intelligent control into one controllable power system — running islanded or tied to the utility. For remote operations it replaces a costly, fragile dependency: diesel logistics, outage exposure and tariff volatility.

PV + BESS + controls Islanded or grid-tied Load-profile matched Remote monitoring
Why It Matters

The necessity case

  • Mine and wellpad sites often sit beyond economic grid extension
  • Diesel power ties operating cost to fuel logistics and price swings
  • Outages stop production — storage bridges them instantly
  • Sustainability targets demand measurable renewable share
What We Deliver

Technology plus everything around it

As an EPCC contractor, we deliver the Huawei microgrid as a working plant — not boxes on a dock. Our scope wraps the technology with the civil, electrical and commissioning work that makes it produce.
