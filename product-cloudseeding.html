Product 05 · Selerys

Cloud seeding

Rainfall, on demand

What It Is

Rainfall as a controllable input

LAICO™ SOBLI is an automatic cloud seeding solution: atmospheric sensing identifies seedable conditions and the system executes autonomously — enhancing rainfall for reservoirs, agriculture and haze mitigation, without waiting on manual flight windows.

Automatic operation Atmospheric sensing Water security Haze & fire mitigation
Why It Matters

Why Indonesia seeds clouds

  • Hydro reservoirs and PLTA catchments need rainfall reliability
  • Agriculture seasons depend on precipitation timing
  • Land and forest fire haze is a recurring national challenge
  • Manual operations are slow — automation reacts in real time
