What It Is

A data center that arrives factory-tested

Huawei's prefabricated, modular data center infrastructure — power distribution, cooling and intelligent monitoring engineered as one factory-tested block, deployed at the edge or at facility scale.

Prefabricated modules Power · cooling · monitoring Fast deployment Edge to enterprise
Why It Matters

Why prefabricated wins

  • Traditional data center builds take years — modules deploy in weeks
  • Factory integration removes field engineering risk
  • Monitoring is built in from day one, not retrofitted
  • Scale by adding modules, not by rebuilding rooms
What We Deliver

Technology plus everything around it

As an EPCC contractor, we deliver the data center facility as a working plant — not boxes on a dock. Our scope wraps the modules with the site engineering, civil and commissioning work that makes them run.
