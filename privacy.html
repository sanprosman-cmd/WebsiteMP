Privacy & Cookie Policy

How PT Maharani Prima collects, uses and protects your data when you visit our website or contact us.

1. Data Controller

PT Maharani Prima, CIBIS Nine Business Park, 11th Floor, Jl. TB Simatupang No.2, Cilandak Timur, Jakarta Selatan 12560, Indonesia. Email: admin@maharaniprima.com.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Contact Form Data

When you submit the contact form, we collect: name, company, email, phone, interest area and message. Your submission is relayed through our hosted form service to our team inbox — nothing is stored on this website's own servers. If the form service cannot be reached, the page instead prepares a pre-filled email draft in your own mail client, so you always see exactly what is sent.

2.2 Analytics Data

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics 4 collects: anonymised IP address, page views, scroll depth, referral source, device/browser type and approximate geography (city level). We use this to improve the site experience.

2.3 Technical Data

Server logs automatically record: IP address, timestamp, requested URL, HTTP status code and user agent. These are retained for security and debugging for up to 30 days.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR / PDPA Indonesia)

  • Essential cookies: Legitimate interest — required for site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: Your explicit consent via the cookie banner.
  • Contact form data: Contractual necessity — you initiate the request.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Analytics data is processed by Google (Google Analytics 4) under their Data Processing Amendment. Contact form submissions are relayed by our hosted form service, which acts as a data processor on our behalf and delivers the submission to our mailbox — it is not used for any other purpose.

6. Data Retention

  • Contact emails: retained in our mailbox per business record-keeping requirements (7 years).
  • Analytics data: 14 months (GA4 default), then automatically deleted.
  • Server logs: 30 days.
  • Cookie consent record: 1 year.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR and Indonesia PDPA (UU PDP), you have the right to: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email admin@maharaniprima.com.

8. International Transfers

Analytics data may be processed on Google servers outside Indonesia. Google provides Standard Contractual Clauses and is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures: HTTPS/TLS 1.3, secure headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options), no client-side storage of personal data beyond the consent cookie, and regular dependency updates.

10. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Material changes will be highlighted via the cookie banner.
