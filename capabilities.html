Core Competency

Five disciplines. One accountable contractor.

From bulk earthworks to energization — the full EPCC chain under one contract, one schedule and one safety standard.

01 · Mining & Hauling

Moving earth at production scale

More than 3.6 million m³ of fresh soil and gravel moved across active mining concessions — with the survey discipline to prove every cubic meter.

  • Excavation, deep bulk excavation & hauling
  • Ore prediction, surveys & volumetric calculation
  • Road, access & facility maintenance
  • Re-engineering and stabilizing fills
  • Loading & barging
02 · Wellpad & Earth Works

Rig-ready ground, on drilling schedule

220 rig substructures installed, 220 wellpads prepared and 300 km of wellpad roads maintained — built to receive the rig on the day the drilling program demands.

  • Excavation, deep bulk excavation & hauling
  • Surveys & volumetric calculation
  • Road, access & facility maintenance
  • Piling, rig foundation & mud pit installation
  • Re-engineering and stabilizing fills
03 · Surface Facility Oil & Gas

From plot plan to commissioning

Metering stations, gas facilities and crude handling — engineered, procured, constructed and commissioned under one accountable scope.

  • Engineering and calculation
  • Earth works and civil works
  • Technical procurement & installation
  • Piping construction and mechanical installation
  • Electrical & instrument installation
  • Testing & commissioning
04 · HV & MV Transmission Line

550 kilometers of energized grid

410 km underground MV, 80 km overhead MV/HV and 60 km HDD cable routes — including 150 kV tower foundations and substation integration.

  • Tower foundation of 20 kV & 150 kV power line
  • Cable pulling and accessories installation
  • Open cut, manual boring & HDD cable installation
  • Cable & panel termination, testing & commissioning
  • Panel, SCADA and electric breaker installation
05 · Power Plant

Nine plants. Every technology class.

Coal, gas, diesel, mini-hydro and geothermal — full EPC scope from engineering through rotating equipment installation to testing & commissioning.

  • Engineering & calculation
  • Earth works and civil works
  • Technical procurement & installation
  • Mechanical & rotating equipment installation
  • Electrical & instrument installation
  • Testing & commissioning
