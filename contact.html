Frequently Asked

Questions we hear often

What does EPCC mean and how is it different from EPC?

EPCC stands for Engineering, Procurement, Construction & Commissioning. The extra "C" covers commissioning — testing, energization, performance guarantees and operator training — so the plant runs before we hand it over. We own the schedule from first calculation to final signature.

Which sectors and regions do you serve?

Power generation (coal, gas, diesel, hydro, geothermal), oil & gas surface facilities, HV/MV transmission, wellpad construction and mining & hauling — across 28 active locations in Sumatra, Java, Kalimantan, Sulawesi, NTB, NTT, Maluku and Papua.

What licenses and certifications do you hold?

SBUJK (construction), SBUJPTL (electrical), SKUP Migas, EBTKE, ISO 9001:2015, ISO 14001:2015, ISO 45001:2018, ISO 37001:2016. Registered vendor for PLN, Pertamina, PGN, Indonesia Power, PJB, Wijaya Karya and Brantas Abipraya.

How do you handle safety on remote sites?

Our HSSE system is ISO 45001 certified. We achieved 5,402,696 safe man-hours without lost injury (Pertamina Gas 2025 award). Every site gets a dedicated HSE officer, daily toolbox talks, permit-to-work systems and real-time incident reporting.

Can you provide a budget and schedule before we commit?

Yes. Share the scope, location and target dates — we'll return a preliminary execution plan, resource histogram and Class 3–4 estimate within 5 business days. No obligation.
